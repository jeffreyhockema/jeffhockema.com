Retro Game Browser

Your LaunchBox library, in any web browser, playable from anywhere, with friends.

Turns the LaunchBox collection on a Windows PC into a website: box art, videos and manuals for the whole library, and a Play button that runs the game in the browser through EmulatorJS, a WebAssembly build of MAME, js-dos and ScummVM. Send a friend a link and they're in, with rollback netcode for the consoles and over 1,300 arcade games, video streaming for the rest, and IPX LAN play for 255 DOS games. Offline copies, sign-in, an admin page, and a Cloudflare Tunnel to reach it from anywhere.

  • Node.js server
  • EmulatorJS · MAME 0.244 · js-dos · ScummVM
  • WebRTC rollback netplay
  • Up to 4 players
  • Windows installer
  • GPL-3.0

jLooper

Play something and it loops. Play again and it layers.

Eight tracks of built-in synth voices, TR-707, TR-808 and TR-909 drum kits, or a live mic. The first loop you record sets the tempo and becomes the master loop, every other loop is a whole multiple of it, and overdubs stack on top with scenes for alternate takes. The engine is native code compiled to WebAssembly running inside an AudioWorklet, shared with the desktop app along with its .looper session format. The on-screen Launchkey is drawn to scale and every pad and button is clickable.

  • Web Audio · AudioWorklet
  • WebAssembly engine
  • 8 tracks · scenes · overdub
  • Built-in voices + mic input
  • MIDI controller support

TDDL

A top-down Doom-like shooter in plain JavaScript, with no build step.

Procedural levels with red, yellow and blue key doors in order, secret walls, switches, chain-exploding barrels, and lava, waste and water. The monsters are Doom's own, with their original stats and AI ported from id's p_enemy.c: they wake to sight or sound, chase in eight-direction zig-zags, and turn on each other in crossfire. A flashlight cone and ray-cast shadows decide what you see, seven weapons share Doom's ammo pools, and a boss seals the exit until it's dead.

  • Vanilla JS · ES modules
  • HTML5 Canvas
  • Procedural levels
  • Doom monster AI
  • Dynamic lighting
  • Web Audio
