Audio · Live demo

jLooper

A live-looping instrument in the browser. Play something and it loops; play again and it layers. Built on the Web Audio API with an AudioWorklet engine and classic TR-707, TR-808 and TR-909 drum kits, all drawn on a single canvas.

Open jLooper /jlooper
Game · Live demo

TDDL

A top-down Doom-like shooter written in plain JavaScript. Find the red, yellow and blue keys, push through locked doors, and reach the exit before the level's boss does you in. Flashlight visibility, secret walls, seven weapons, and sound synthesised on the Web Audio API.

Play TDDL /tddl
Color · Live demo

RGB

A playground for colour and light. Mix channels, watch the blend change in real time, and see how red, green and blue add up to everything on screen. Runs on its own subdomain so it can stay simple and fast.

Open RGB rgb.jeffhockema.com
