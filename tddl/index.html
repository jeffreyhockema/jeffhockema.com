How to Play

Objective

• Reach the exit. You need not kill everything, but a boss seals the exit until it is dead.

• Keys open doors of their colour, in order: red to yellow to blue to the exit. Push into a locked door with its key to open it for good.

Staying Alive

• Your flashlight lights a cone ahead. Past it you make out grey shapes in a wide arc in front, and nothing behind you.

• Enemies sleep until they see you or hear a shot. Caught in each other's fire, they turn on each other.

• Green barrels chain-explode. Lava kills fast, waste burns slowly, water slows you. None is ever the only way through.

• The pistol and rifle share bullets; the plasma gun and BFG share cells.

Secrets

• Some walls are not walls: push into them. A switch is pressed by walking into it or shooting it, and slides a wall aside nearby.
